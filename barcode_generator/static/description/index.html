Auto-Barcode Generator

Auto-Barcode Generator

This module enables you to generate automatic barcode to streamline inventory management, by letting you set up a prefix based on product categories. Efficient product labeling helps to maintain inventory accuracy.

Description

Description

Experience the power of our custom 'Auto Barcode Generator' module, developed to meet your unique requirements. You can assign pre-defined prefixes on the basis of product categories. Inventory tracking and management are made easier in a significant way by this essential tool. This customized module, in contrast to Odoo's default capabilities, automates the barcode generation such that each product will have a unique barcode. This essential addition will help your company track and manage inventory more effectively, reducing the amount of labor that needs to be done by hand and greatly improving accuracy. Our unique "Auto Barcode Generator" module will improve your inventory management because it was created to bridge a significant hole in Odoo's built-in features.

In addition, this module simplifies the process of assigning unique barcodes based on product categories, allowing you to create a structured and organized inventory. With the ability to set a distinct barcode prefix consisting of three fixed digits, followed by automatically generated random digits, this module facilitates easy product identification. These three fixed initial digits provide a mnemonic reference, making it effortless for users to recall and locate products based on their barcodes. Streamline your inventory tracking and management with precision and efficiency.

Standout Features

Standout Features

  • Available on Community and Enterprise versions on Odoo 14,15,16 Editions.
  • Streamline Inventory Management: Simplify the inventory control process by implementing barcode scanning and seamless product labeling.
  • Automatic Barcode Generation: Say goodbye to manual barcode creation. Our module automates the process, saving you time and reducing the risk of errors.
  • Enhanced Inventory Tracking: The automated barcode generation improves inventory tracking accuracy, making it easier to monitor stock levels, sales, and product movements.
  • Three-Digit Prefix for each product category: Users can assign a three-digit fixed prefix to the barcode, providing a structured reference point for easy product identification.
  • Efficient Product Categorization: It provides a well-organized inventory management by making the process of allocating unique barcodes based on up to 99 product categories simpler. Users can easily choose a reference point for simple product identification by applying a structured, three-digit fixed prefix to the barcode.

Exploring the module: (How to)

Exploring the module: (How to)

  • Download and Install the module.
  • After enabling "auto-barcode generator", system will allow you to create the 3-digits numeric barcode prefix on the product categories.
  • Create a new "Product" and assign a same product category on which you have assign a Barcode Prefix.
  • After selecting a product category on a Product, system will auto generate a unique barcode for the specific product.
