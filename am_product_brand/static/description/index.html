POS Product Brand

Organize and filter products by brand in Point of Sale

Overview

This module extends Odoo Point of Sale by adding comprehensive brand management capabilities. Create, manage, and organize your products by brand, then filter them easily in the POS interface for faster product selection.

Use Case: Perfect for retail stores that carry multiple brands (such as electronics stores, clothing shops, or supermarkets) and want to quickly filter and find products by brand during sales.

Key Features

✔ Brand Management - Create and manage multiple product brands with images, codes, and descriptions
✔ POS Brand Filtering - Filter products by brand directly in the POS interface for faster product selection
✔ POS Configuration - Enable/disable brand filtering and configure available brands per POS point
✔ Brand Images - Display brand logos in the POS selector for easy visual identification
✔ Product Count - View the number of products assigned to each brand at a glance

How It Works

Step 1: Go to Point of Sale > Products > Brands and create your product brands with images and descriptions
Step 2: Assign brands to your products in the product form (Inventory > Products)
Step 3: Enable brand filtering in your POS configuration settings and select available brands
Step 4: Use the brand selector in POS to filter products by brand during sales
