Frequently asked

Why does this exist as a separate module?

Every paid Arure connector needs the same plumbing — a job queue, an audit log, an instance registry, a mapping engine. Splitting it out means each paid connector has half the code, the same operational behaviour, and one place to fix shared bugs.

Do I need this if I'm not buying any paid connector?

No. By itself it does nothing user-facing. It's a foundation library. Install it as a dependency of any Arure paid connector.

Why no queue_job dependency?

queue_job adds an OCA dependency, requires extra workers, and breaks on Odoo Online. Our background processor uses Odoo's standard cron + a state-machine table, with a two-commit pattern that guarantees a job is either marked running or marked failed before any external call.

Will this work on Odoo Online (SaaS)?

No. Per Odoo's policy, third-party apps cannot be installed on Odoo Online. This module runs on Odoo.sh and on-premise (self-hosted Community or Enterprise) deployments.

Why is it free?

Because charging for foundation infrastructure that every paid connector requires would be charging twice. The paid connectors carry the price; the foundation is shared overhead.

Frequently asked

Do I need this module on its own?

No. It's a foundation library. Install it indirectly — any Arure connector (WooCommerce, FedEx, Klaviyo, BI) lists it as a dependency, so Odoo pulls it in automatically.

What does it actually provide?

A background-job queue with exponential-backoff retry, a structured audit log, a per-instance operation queue, and a base field-mapping framework. The paid connectors call into these so their codebases stay small and their failure modes consistent.

Why is it free?

Because making customers pay for a dependency they didn't ask for is bad design. The paid connectors fund the maintenance of this layer.

Will this work on Odoo Online (SaaS)?

No. Per Odoo's policy, third-party apps cannot be installed on Odoo Online. This module runs on Odoo.sh and on-premise (self-hosted Community or Enterprise) deployments.

Does it survive Odoo upgrades?

Yes. Bug fixes and security patches are committed for 12 months after Odoo 21 ships.

What happens if I uninstall?

Models and menus added by the module are removed. Past data created through dependent connectors remains in place. No migration required.
