# Support Framework — Connector Common
**Module:** Connector Common (free) · Arure Technologies
**Effective:** 2026-05-06

This is the free foundation module. Support details mirror the paid
connectors that depend on it.

## Channels

| Channel | Purpose | Response SLA |
|---|---|---|
| `support@arure.tech` | Bug reports, anything needing an audit trail | 1 business day |
| WhatsApp `+1 858 463 4405` | Quick configuration / sanity checks | Same business day, Mon–Fri 09:00–19:00 IST best-effort |
| Odoo App Store reviews | Public ratings | Reply within 7 business days |

## Severity SLAs

| Severity | Definition | Acknowledgement | Diagnosis | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 — Critical | Module installation fails on Odoo 19 | 1 business day | 3 days | next minor release |
| S2 — Major | A documented feature crashes | 1 day | 5 days | next minor release |
| S3 — Minor | Cosmetic / workaround available | 2 days | 10 days | next minor release |
| S4 — Question | "How do I..." | 2 days | 5 days | n/a |

## Coverage

This is a free module. Bug fixes are prioritized when they affect a
paid connector that depends on this foundation. Standalone feature
requests are evaluated case-by-case but lower priority than paid module
issues.

## Maintenance commitment

- **Until 12 months after Odoo 21 ships** (currently: until ~Sep 2027 at earliest).
- Or **until 18 months after the last paid connector that depends on this module is sold**, whichever is later.

## What support does NOT cover

- Migrations between Odoo major versions
- Custom development
- Performance tuning of your Odoo or PostgreSQL infrastructure
- Issues in third-party Odoo modules outside the Arure portfolio
