About
============
Wan Buffer Services's Terms & Conditions module helps businesses clearly communicate their policies on sales and purchases. With this module, you can easily create, manage, and display custom terms and conditions on quotations, sale orders, purchase orders, and invoices. The module supports rich text formatting and multi-company environments.

Key Features
============
- Create and manage multiple terms and conditions for sales and purchases.
- Assign terms and conditions to specific companies (multi-company support).
- Set default terms for each customer or vendor.
- Option to show or hide terms and conditions on printed documents.
- User-friendly HTML editor for formatting.

Installation
============
1. Copy the module folder to your Odoo addons directory.
2. Restart your Odoo service.
3. Activate developer mode in Odoo.
4. Update the app list and search for "Terms and Conditions".
5. Install the module.
