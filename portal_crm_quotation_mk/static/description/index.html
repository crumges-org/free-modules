Complete CRM and Quotation Management from Odoo Portal

Odoo 17.0 Portal Ready Mobile Friendly

📋 Overview

Transform your sales workflow by allowing employees to manage CRM Leads/Opportunities and create Sales Quotations directly from the Portal interface. This powerful module eliminates the need for full Odoo user access, enabling your sales team to work efficiently from anywhere.

💡 Key Benefit: Empower your sales team with full CRM capabilities through the portal, reducing licensing costs while maintaining productivity.
